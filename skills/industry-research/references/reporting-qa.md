# Reporting and actual checks

Maintain two mappings in report-map (which may be a section in a merged file): the answer/gap disposition for every user question, and C to body/charts and summary. Derive reader citations from source records; do not recopy source URLs, locators, or full claims into the mapping table. Question coverage does not make every conclusion usable; do not silently remove questions because evidence is insufficient.

Write the summary after the body stabilizes. Every summary item must point back to the body and C, without new claims, omitted material conditions, or upgraded confidence. Titles, legends, axes, colors, positions, rankings, and takeaways also express conclusions and must match the actual data fields; technical strength must not imply commercial maturity.

Give readers understandable source titles/links and necessary locators; keep internal IDs in traceability material. Removing internal audit terminology must not remove limitations. Show unknown dates honestly. Actually check whether a citation supports its sentence; a clickable link is not sufficient.

## Delivery checks

- Check evidence, contrary evidence, qualifiers, and every occurrence of key C; recompute important numbers, check question coverage, and align the summary.
- For charts, check semantics and embedded text. For paginated output, inspect actual renders for clipping, overlap, fonts, page numbers, blank pages, and readability at physical size. Text extraction/source scans cannot replace visual inspection.
- Script-generated PASS, preset notes, or old QA are not evidence of current checks. Record files/pages actually viewed, actions, and findings. If viewing is unavailable, retain NOT REVIEWED and state the delivery limitation.
- After fixing something, recheck affected outputs. Do not claim uninspected artifacts or pages passed. Unchanged source data do not guarantee unchanged rendering.

Ordinary text delivery does not require a PDF. A project without visual artifacts may mark visual checks `不适用` (not applicable), with a reason. A visual artifact awaiting delivery but not viewed must not be marked inapplicable to evade inspection.
