# Optional analysis

Load only applicable parts; interviews, funding databases, and models are not mandatory.

## Commercial evidence and parameters

Allow commercial parameters directly supported by evidence, including scope-matched signed contract amounts and amounts paid, with their actual meaning stated. Record award, signing, delivery, payment, revenue recognition, and renewal separately; do not substitute one for another. Check the procurement object, parties, period, scope, tax/currency, and measurement basis. A software-service contract does not prove hardware procurement; one payment does not directly establish annual revenue or a renewal rate.

Technical evidence does not substitute for commercial returns, financing for customer demand, quotations for transactions, or wages for full costs. Do not deny a documented payment merely because renewal evidence is absent. Assess whether commercial material is first-hand separately from whether it supports a particular parameter.

## Quantification and scenarios

Record P parameters and A assumptions: ID, `observed` / `proxy` / `assumption`, S/E basis or assumption rationale, unit, conditional denominator, scope, formula/upstream/downstream dependencies, and applicability limits. Only sufficiently evidenced parameters may be labeled observed; do not present a proxy as a measurement.

Recompute key outputs. Check for repeatedly multiplied shares, consistency of stocks/flows and periods, and plausible implied quantities. Review sample representativeness separately from arithmetic. `Low` / `Reference` / `High` are scenarios, not automatically probabilities; do not report confidence intervals without calibration. With insufficient inputs, provide qualitative results or explicitly assumed, limited sensitivity analysis; do not extrapolate a registry into the actual whole-market size.

## User-provided interviews

Confirm permitted analysis, quotation, attribution, and publication separately; do not conflate permissions. Prefer original notes/transcripts. An interview guide is not testimony, and assumptions in questions are not respondent facts. Separate atomic observations into first-hand operational, first-hand commercial, expert opinion, second-hand knowledge, and speculation; retain contrary evidence.

Role, direct information access, region, period, denominator, and independence determine what can be supported, not headcount or observation-row count alone. A technical expert's authority cannot calibrate commercial parameters outside their direct knowledge; direct commercial documents may be used within their scope. Do not invent timestamps or infer geography from language or nationality.
