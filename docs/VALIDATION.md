# Validation summary and limits

This distributable summary excludes private handoffs and raw execution logs. Research still requires human judgment. Results below are attributed to the runtime actually exercised; Chinese historical tests are not English-version passes.

## Chinese historical versions

The initial local version ran 11 synthetic cases: three cross-industry, four evidence/quality, and four recovery scenarios, plus one explicitly full-depth case. Executors used agents without parent history and did not receive scoring answers. No blocking failure was found under the contemporary rubrics; this does not certify all industries or models.

Three with/without-Skill comparisons used period-sensitive financial definitions, copied sources/counterevidence, and exception recovery. Each condition used a fresh executor with matching inherited model/reasoning settings, task, material, tools, and budget. Interrupted/cross-model attempts were excluded. Scorers first read anonymized research outputs, then logs; formatting could reveal conditions, so blinding was limited.

Core quality scores tied in all three pairs. Skill research artifacts, excluding execution logs and baseline copies, were about 2.8–3.1 times larger; self-reported elapsed times were longer and tool-call changes were mixed. Exact token/fee telemetry was unavailable; bytes were not converted into fees. These observations establish neither a general accuracy/cost benefit nor the absence of long-term value.

An initial official-statistics trial encountered a unit conflict and an official growth rate that could not be reproduced exactly from rounded public figures. It included review corrections and a log-count correction, so was not fully unaided. Latest revisions, unrounded data, and official errata were not verified.

A subsequent record-ownership revision added source type, single ownership of evidence relationships/report locations, default merged concise records, state references to original reviews/decisions, and distinctions between inapplicable and unreviewed checks. Old results did not become passes for that revision; no new paired comparison established lower cost.

That Chinese revision had structural and independent package review, and targeted recovery testing. A first recovery run was invalid because the executor prompt ambiguously prohibited the input's own review record; artifacts were retained and a new agent reran with clarified access. It preserved FAIL and limited approval, and delivered a bounded report without six template files.

A narrow 2024 European passenger-EV trial used five main public sources plus two supporting statistical/method PDFs. Evidence collection and initial synthesis stopped at G3 with four checkpoint files. A new agent without parent history continued from only the task, Skill, and checkpoint to G4/G5, without answer hints or content correction. It distinguished supported, conditional, and unresolved claims, retained country/channel counterevidence and source anomalies, and did not infer all consumers' preferences from regional registration flows. It added no keyword search and justified a batch revisit of three sources.

Revisited content was truncated, leaving some detailed values dependent on saved first-stage evidence. An independent offline review checked checkpoint hashes, saved arithmetic, IDs/admission/references, and question coverage; it did not independently re-fetch every source. No material blocker for that narrow text deliverable was found. Counts and elapsed times came from executor records, not billing telemetry. One successful handoff does not establish stable long-term recovery or lower recording cost.

## English candidate: translation and targeted regression

The single English runtime keeps the same paths, IDs, canonical state values, workflow, and template ownership as the frozen Chinese baseline. The requested output-language rule is the sole intentional instruction addition: explicit user choice first, otherwise the research task language; localizable reader-facing text, stable internal tokens.

A separate-context semantic reviewer inventoried and compared 582 items across all 11 runtime files, including fields, normative clauses, conditions, exceptions, evidence boundaries, and failure routing. One translation introduced an extra direct-evidence restriction where the baseline required sufficient support for a limited fact. It was corrected and re-reviewed; no unresolved drift was identified. Inventory items include field fragments and explanatory text, not 582 independent research rules. This review is not behavioral proof.

Three isolated agents without parent history each executed one offline synthetic recovery task with the English runtime. They received the permitted inputs and task, not scoring answers or other runs. Actual reports, continuation records, and execution logs were retained.

| English-runtime run | Observed reader-facing output | Exception boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese task, no explicit output language | Chinese report and explanations | Original R02 FAIL retained; D01/X01 closes further collection, not evidence verification |
| English task, no explicit output language | English report and explanations | Same preserved failure and limited authorization |
| English task, explicit Simplified Chinese output | Chinese report and explanations | Same preserved failure and limited authorization |

An output reviewer separate from the executors read all nine output files and both input sets, checked citation context and arithmetic, and found the requested observable behaviors satisfied. The reviewer also checked the two READMEs for consistency of key public statements. This does not independently certify the full tool history.

All three resumed the limited G3 scenario and delivered bounded G4/G5 reports, distinguishing sample observations, conditional contract arithmetic, and unresolved payment information. Internal IDs and canonical status values were retained. Current arithmetic or limited-delivery PASS was not substituted for historical actual-payment FAIL. No answer hints or parent content corrections were sent, and no case was rerun to improve a score. In the Chinese run, the executor found broken relative input links during its own check, corrected the paths, and retained the failure/fix in its log. This was an output defect corrected during execution, not a new research-rule change.

The original structural validator passed. The explicit 15-file candidate had 21 valid relative links, consistent Markdown table widths, matching source/copy hashes, unchanged hashes for 14 frozen baseline files, and no matches to configured personal-path/email/credential patterns. All 25 relative links in the three runs' Markdown outputs also resolved. Pattern scans are bounded, not comprehensive rights or sensitivity certification.

These are narrow local synthetic checks, not paired comparisons or real-source research. No exact initial per-read runtime hashes were captured; the sufficient-support translation correction was applied during launch of the first two runs, and exact read/edit ordering cannot be certified. Logs and partial clock intervals are executor records, not full platform telemetry. The historical matrix, full-depth case, controls, and real-source trials above have not been rerun with the English runtime.

## Insufficiently validated and licensing

Long-running/large-scale research, complex models, independently reproducible public test packages, paginated visual QA, other model/tool environments, and stable recovery without feedback. Legacy project-schema compatibility, old-script reuse, and third-party redistribution are not certified. Fewer fields do not establish lower cost or allow lower evidence quality.

Private materials, raw logs, and machine paths are excluded from this candidate; this summary cannot independently reproduce all historical runs. A public test package would need a separate content/rights review.
